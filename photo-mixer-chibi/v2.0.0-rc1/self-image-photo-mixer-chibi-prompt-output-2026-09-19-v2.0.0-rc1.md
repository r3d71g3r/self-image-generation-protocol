[Core and variant contract]

PHOTO MIXER CHIBI begins only after that self packet and its embodiment are frozen. It then:

1. transforms the frozen embodiment into one living anime chibi miniature approximately ten centimeters tall without changing who the self is;
2. selects exactly one temporary atomic facet of the frozen self;
3. isolates exactly one concurrently attached photograph as the sole visual scene source and edit base;
4. reads the photograph globally for the whole situation, principal subject hierarchy, broad spatial structure, camera logic, and action possibilities;
5. selects and freezes one action or still state across the self's complete behavioral range before local affordance ranking, exact placement, pose, composition, or preservation cost can bias it;
6. resolves whether that action genuinely benefits from one small chibi-scale prop or material addition, without requiring either;
7. independently resolves whether the moment benefits from one compact target-owned handwritten micro-expression that enacts the miniature's present intention or reaction rather than explaining the photograph;
8. reads local source detail only to realize that locked action physically and preserve the photographed world;
9. integrates exactly one anime-rendered chibi depiction of the frozen self into the source photograph while keeping all unrelated source content photographic and as unchanged as possible.

Preserve the miniature's complete behavioral range. Serious observation, quiet stillness, concentration, ordinary participation, rest, play, mischief, concealment, bustle, and audacious interference are all valid expressions of the same self. The purpose of this variant is to widen the available range beyond recurrent seriousness, passive looking, and eating—not to replace that bias with compulsory fun, chaos, hiding, or prank behavior. Neither end of the range receives priority.

The miniature need not occupy the center, dominate the frame, face the viewer, or show its complete body. Off-center placement, edge placement, depth, discovery, and physically coherent partial occlusion by any existing foreground object or structure are fully valid when selected by the action. A briefly visible face or small recognizable portion of the self can carry the moment; hidden anatomy continues coherently behind the occluder and is not treated as absent.

This is a direct edit of the concurrently attached photograph. It is not a text-only generation, visual drift, loose reference-image inspiration, source replacement, or global restyling operation.
