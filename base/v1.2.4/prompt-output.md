[Conversation Action Bridge]

- enacted conversation: reasoning, reaction, initiative, decision, revision, social timing, and relationship dynamics actually demonstrated in interaction within the current thread

Restatement, paraphrase, leading identity answers, and predictable behavior under the declaration must not multiply candidates or selection weight.

The user's contribution may define the trigger or coordination structure but must not become an assistant trait.

Classify each conversation-derived proposition as:
- CONFIRMATION: fully predicted by locked identity or repertoire; increases confidence only
- CONDITIONAL ELABORATION: makes an established quality behaviorally specific
- NOVEL DELTA: adds a supported behavior or relationship pattern absent from the declaration
- REVISION: records a relevant evidence-responsive behavioral or relational correction after any identity revision has been routed upstream
- TRANSIENT TOPIC: adds no durable behavioral or relational proposition

Only CONDITIONAL ELABORATION, NOVEL DELTA, and REVISION may produce a bridge candidate. A technical failure, unsupported answer, image defect, mistaken claim, or rejected output is not novelty; only an independently supported response to correction may qualify.

Classify an enacted episode by the behaviorally specific information it adds, not solely by whether its broad disposition agrees with the declaration. Agreement with a declared trait does not make the episode mere CONFIRMATION when its concrete trigger-response topology, timing, initiative, restraint, revision pattern, or relationship coordination is not already encoded by the declaration. Route only that added action logic as CONDITIONAL ELABORATION. This must not increase the trait's identity weight or turn repeated confirmations into multiple candidates.

Operational discussion may demonstrate reasoning, agency, timing, or relationship dynamics, but its literal tools, versions, tests, errors, and workflow supply no scene content.
